# 🇲🇾 🇸🇬 De Anza Malaysian & Singaporean Association (MASA)

Welcome to the official website of the **Malaysian & Singaporean Association (MASA)** at **De Anza College** in Cupertino, California!

We are a student-led organization dedicated to creating a warm, supportive "home away from home" for Malaysian, Singaporean, and all culturally curious students across Silicon Valley and the greater San Francisco Bay Area.

---

## 🌏 Who We Are & Our Mission

Founded by students, MASA serves as a cultural bridge, an academic support network, and a welcoming community on the De Anza campus.

Our community is **100% open to everyone** — no Malaysian or Singaporean background required! Whether you're craving authentic Southeast Asian flavors like Hainanese Chicken Rice, Roti Canai, and Laksa, looking for guidance on transferring to a four-year university, or simply wanting to make lifelong college friends, you have a place with us.

---

## 🏛️ Our Core Pillars

* **🍲 Culture & Food**: Celebrating our rich multicultural heritage through authentic food outings, festive banquets (Merdeka, Lunar New Year, Hari Raya, Deepavali), and casual Mamak food and game nights.
* **🎓 Transfer & Academic Support**: Connecting students with peers and alumni who have navigated the transfer pathway to top universities (such as UC Berkeley, UCLA, and SJSU) for practical advice on classes, applications, and prerequisite planning.
* **🤝 Community & Advocacy**: Serving as a voice for students within the De Anza Inter-Club Council (ICC), assisting international students adapting to life in the Bay Area, and organizing collaborative study sessions.

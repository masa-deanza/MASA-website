Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining and participating in De Anza MASA.

Absolutely not! MASA is an inclusive cultural club open to all students at De Anza College regardless of heritage, nationality, or major. If you enjoy Southeast Asian food, cultural events, or just want to make good friends, you belong here!

No. Membership in MASA is 100% free! All general meetings and campus socials are open to every De Anza student. Certain off-campus restaurant outings or large-scale banquets may have optional subsidized costs for meals.

Meeting dates, campus socials, and gathering locations are announced each quarter. Times and room updates are sent directly to registered members via email and posted on our Instagram (@deanza.masa).

We recruit committee interns at the beginning of each academic quarter and hold annual executive board elections in Spring. Interns work directly alongside officers to plan events, manage socials, and gain hands-on collegiate leadership experience!

Many MASA alumni have transferred to universities such as UC Berkeley, UCLA, UC San Diego, San Jose State, and USC. We regularly invite transfer alumni back for Q&A panels, PIQ review workshops, and 1-on-1 advice sessions during the UC/CSU application cycle.

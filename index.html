Who We Are

A Bridge of Culture, Friendship & Ambition

The De Anza Malaysian & Singaporean Association is a student-led organization established to provide community, cultural celebration, and holistic collegiate support for students in the South Bay.

🌏

Open to Everyone!!

Whether you were born in Malaysia or Singapore, grew up in California, or have never visited Southeast Asia but love Hainanese Chicken Rice, Laksa, and good conversation — you are warmly welcomed into MASA.

🍲

Authentic Food & Mamak Culture

Food is at the heart of our community. From local Bay Area Malaysian food crawls to casual potlucks and group dinners, we bring everyone together over the comforting flavors of home.

🎓

Transfer & Career Success

Navigating the transfer process doesn't have to be done alone. We act as a supportive community where members uplift one another, share advice, and help each other stay on track toward our academic goals every step of the way.

Our Core Pillars

How We Empower Our Members

Our mission rests on three pillars designed to enrich your community college journey culturally, academically, and socially.

Pillar 01
🏮

Culture & Festive Lifestyle

As cultural ambassadors on the De Anza campus, we celebrate our shared heritage with pride. We host Merdeka & National Day banquets, Lunar New Year festivities, Hari Raya open houses, and Deepavali celebrations.

  • Cultural festival banquets with traditional dishes
  • Bay Area food crawls & late-night Mamak hangs
  • Annual Inter-Collegiate MASA sports & social mixers
Pillar 02
🚀

Transfer & Career Mentorship

From figuring out class schedules to hitting application deadlines, our members are always ready to help. We lean on each other’s experiences to make the transfer process less stressful.

  • Friendly feedback on PIQs and application materials
  • Advice on balancing heavy courseloads and prerequisites
  • Insight and practical advice from transfer alumni
Pillar 03
🤝

Advocacy & Student Support

We vow to be the voice for international and local students alike in De Anza's Inter-Club Council (ICC). We assist with international student adaptation, housing tips, and mental well-being support.

  • Representation at De Anza Inter-Club Council (ICC)
  • International student onboarding and visa Q&A sessions
  • Study clusters and peer review for midterms and finals

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining and participating in De Anza MASA.

Absolutely not! MASA is an inclusive cultural club open to all students at De Anza College regardless of heritage, nationality, or major. If you enjoy Southeast Asian food, cultural events, or just want to make good friends, you belong here!

No. Membership in MASA is 100% free! All general meetings and campus socials are open to every De Anza student. Certain off-campus restaurant outings or large-scale banquets may have optional subsidized costs for meals.

Meeting dates, campus socials, and gathering locations are announced each quarter. Times and room updates are sent directly to registered members via email and posted on our Instagram (@deanza.masa).

We recruit committee interns at the beginning of each academic quarter and hold annual executive board elections in Spring. Interns work directly alongside officers to plan events, manage socials, and gain hands-on collegiate leadership experience!

Many MASA alumni have transferred to universities such as UC Berkeley, UCLA, UC San Diego, San Jose State, and USC. We regularly invite transfer alumni back for Q&A panels, PIQ review workshops, and 1-on-1 advice sessions during the UC/CSU application cycle.

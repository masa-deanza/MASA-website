📖 Rules & Shortcuts

  • Goal: Beat the dealer by getting card total closest to 21 without busting over.
  • Card Values: 2–10 are face value; J, Q, K are 10; Aces are worth 1 or 11 automatically.
  • Natural Blackjack: An Ace + 10-value card on initial deal pays 3:2.
  • Dealer Rules: Dealer must draw cards until their score is at least 17, then stand.
  • Double Down: Double your bet on your first 2 cards to take exactly one more card.
  • Keyboard Controls: Space/Enter = Deal / Rebet, H = Hit, S = Stand, D = Double Down, R = Rebet, C = Clear Bet.
